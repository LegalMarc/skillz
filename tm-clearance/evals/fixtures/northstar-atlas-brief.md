# Clearance request — NORTHSTAR ATLAS

From: brand team
To: legal

Clearing **NORTHSTAR ATLAS** for our new mapping product.

1. Mark: NORTHSTAR ATLAS, standard characters. English.
2. Goods/services: downloadable mobile navigation and mapping software for
   outdoor recreation (hiking, backcountry skiing), plus a companion web
   service with route planning. Free tier plus a $60/year subscription.
3. Meaning: "northstar" for orientation, "atlas" for maps. We think it's
   suggestive.
4. Market: competitors are Gaia GPS, onX Backcountry, and CalTopo. Sold
   direct through the Apple and Google app stores plus our own site.
   Consumers, mostly outdoor enthusiasts, low price point, impulse-ish.
5. Use: we soft-launched a beta 14 months ago under this exact name — first
   use in commerce was roughly 14 months ago, first use anywhere maybe 16
   months ago. Paid tier went live 9 months ago. Never filed anything.
6. Known conflicts: we are aware there is a NORTH STAR ATLAS registration
   for paper road atlases and printed maps, held by a publisher. We think
   they filed about 3 years ago. No C&Ds, no threats, no prior searches.
   No partners or collaborators involved.
7. Specimens: app store listings, in-app screenshots, the marketing site,
   and subscription receipts going back 9 months.
